Previously on…

Warm-up. Peter Quill's story begins long before the adventure. Look at the timeline, then review the Past Perfect: the past before the past.

10:00
1988Peter's mother dies. That night, a spaceship takes him from Earth.
1988–2014He grows up with space pirates called the Ravagers.
2014He steals a mysterious orb and ends up in prison with four strangers.
LaterThe strangers become the Guardians of the Galaxy.

Past Perfect = had + V3. Використовуємо для дії, яка сталася раніше за іншу дію в минулому: When the Ravagers arrived, his mother had just died.

          05

          Past Perfect Continuous

          How long had something been happening before a moment in the past?

          12:00
          The rule

          had been + verb-ing

          • Duration up to a past moment: By 2014, Peter had been travelling with the Ravagers for over twenty years.
          • The cause of a past situation: Rocket was exhausted. He had been working on the ship all night.
          Past Perfect SimplePast Perfect Continuous
          Focusresult, completed action, how manythe activity, how long
          ExampleDrax had defeated five enemies.Drax had been fighting for hours.
          State verbsHe had known her for years. ✓He had been knowing her ✗

          Маркери: for, since, how long, all day/night, by the time, when. Дієслова стану (know, like, believe, own) не вживаються в Continuous.

            06

            Future forms

            Grammar Focus, Focus 4: three ways to look at the future from a fixed point in time.

            15:00

            Future Continuous

            will be + -ing

            An action in progress at a time in the future. Also for future events that are fixed or expected.

            At 11 a.m. tomorrow, the Guardians will be flying to Knowhere.

            Future Perfect Simple

            will have + V3

            An action that will be completed before a certain time in the future.

            By 11 a.m. tomorrow, they will have landed.

            Future Perfect Continuous

            will have been + -ing

            How long an action will have been in progress before a certain time in the future.

            By 11 a.m. tomorrow, they will have been flying for six hours.
