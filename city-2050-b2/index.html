          04

          Future forms

          Three ways to look at the future from a fixed point in time.

          15 min

          Future Continuous

          will be + -ing

          1. An action in progress at a time in the future.
          This time next year, we'll be living in a tower block.

          2. Planned or routine future events.
          I'll be going to the shops after school anyway.

          3. Polite questions about plans.
          Will you be using the car tomorrow?

          Future Perfect Simple

          will have + V3

          An action that will be completed before a certain time in the future.

          By 2030, the city will have closed the old car parks.

          They won't have finished the housing development by June.

          Future Perfect Continuous

          will have been + -ing

          How long an action will have been in progress before a certain time in the future.

          By next summer, we'll have been living here for ten years.

          How long will you have been studying English by then?

              Speaking & homework

              Debate the city of the future, then write about your life in it.

              8 min
              Debate
              1. Tower blocks or houses: which is better for families? For teenagers?
              2. Should all cars be banned from city centres by 2040?
              3. Is it fair that some people have a second home while others can't afford one?
              4. Robots will be doing our domestic chores. Will that make us lazier, or give us more time for better things?
              Homework

              A blog post from 2040

              It's 2040. Write a blog post (150–180 words) about your home and your city: where you'll be living, what you'll have done by then, and how long you'll have been living there.
