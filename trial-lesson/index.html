      04

      Reading: Meet Oliver 7 min

      Read about Oliver. Are the sentences true or false? Then find the grammar in the text.

      Hi! I'm Oliver and I'm sixteen. I live in Bristol with my mum, my dad and my little sister, Emma. My mum is a doctor and she works very long hours. My dad has got a small café, so we always have fresh bread at home!

      I go to a big school near the river. My favourite subject is biology because I love nature. I'm not very good at maths – it's harder than any other subject for me.

      At the moment I'm learning Spanish because we are going to visit Spain next summer. I've never been there, but my sister has already been there twice with her school.

      Last weekend was great. On Saturday I played football with my friends, and on Sunday we went to the forest and had a picnic. It rained in the afternoon, but we didn't care.

      In the future, I think I'll be a vet. I have to study a lot, but I'm sure it will be worth it.
